Privacy policy

Updated 22 October 2026

This explains what happens when you use this website.

Fitting bookings

When you book a fitting, your name, email address, occasion, date, time and group size are used to arrange it. The website passes them to Resend, the email service that sends you a confirmation, sends the shop a copy and, for visits up to 30 days away, sends you a reminder the day before. A reminder waits at Resend until it is sent. The website itself does not keep a list of bookings. Your browser also keeps the date, time, group size and occasion so the confirmation page can show them; that copy stays on your device. There is no online checkout or customer account.

Before a booking goes through, Cloudflare Turnstile checks that a person sent it, using your IP address and browser details. Resend, Cloudflare and the receiving email providers handle the booking under their own policies. Read Resend’s privacy policy and Cloudflare’s Turnstile privacy addendum. Do not include payment details or sensitive personal information. Call the shop if you don’t get a confirmation email.

Visits and clicks

The site loads Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity and Google Tag Manager automatically. Analytics can record pages visited, device and browser details, approximate location, how you arrived, and clicks on calls, directions and fitting links. Clarity also records how you scroll, tap and move through pages, as heatmaps and visit replays; what you type into the booking form is masked and not recorded. Cookies may be used to distinguish visits. Your browser sends connection information, including your IP address, to services it loads.

Other services

Google Maps embeds, Google Fonts and Google review links connect to Google. The hosting provider receives requests needed to serve the site and may keep technical logs. These providers handle information under their own policies.

Read Google’s privacy policy, Microsoft’s privacy statement and Vercel’s privacy policy.

Your browser choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The site does not currently have an on-site cookie preference control. See Cookies and website services.

Calling or visiting

Information you provide by phone or in the shop is separate from this website. Ask Gabriel directly about information held by the shop.

Updates and questions

Contact the shop about a booking you have made or to ask about information held in connection with it. This policy will be updated if the website’s services change.
