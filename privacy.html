Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 August 2026

This page explains what happens to your information when you visit gabrielsmenswear.com. The short version: the site does not ask you for anything. There is no form to fill in, no account to make, and nothing to buy here. We use Google Analytics to count visits so we know whether the site is doing its job.

What we do not collect

The site has no contact form, no newsletter signup, no login, and no checkout. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or payment details anywhere on this website, and we have no way to receive them through it.

What is collected automatically

We use Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how people use the site. It sets cookies in your browser and records things like:

  • which pages you looked at, and how long you stayed
  • the type of device and browser you used
  • a rough location, worked out from your IP address, usually no more precise than the town or region
  • how you arrived here, for example from a search engine or a link
  • whether you tapped the call button, the directions link, or the link to our Google reviews

That last one tells us how many people the site sends to the shop. It records that a tap happened, not who did it.

This information is handled by Google as described in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

What we do with it

We look at it in aggregate to see which parts of the site are useful and which are not. We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Turning it off

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Google also publishes a browser add-on that opts you out of Google Analytics on every site you visit. The site works fine either way.

Links to other places

Some links here take you to Google Maps or to our Google Business Profile. Once you are there, that site’s own privacy policy applies, not this one.

Children

This site is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from them.

When we call or fit you

Anything you tell us in the shop or over the phone, such as your measurements, what you are shopping for, or when you need it, is used to do the work you asked for. That happens offline and is not connected to this website.

Changes

If we add something to the site that collects more than this, such as an online booking form, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Questions
