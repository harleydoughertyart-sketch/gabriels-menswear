# Niche targeting reference

Marketing goals per niche, to give a reel a point before you build it. Buyer + before→after language is
locked in `marketing/src/content/copy.useCases.ts` — this file adds the *why it sells* and the *starting
image* so you can aim. Positioning is photoshoot-replacement: **their dull input → catalog-grade output,
one area at a time, the subject never changes.** Add new niches here as you target them.

| Niche | Buyer | Job they're always trying to do | What the reel must prove | Starting "before" image |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ecommerce** | Etsy / Shopify / Amazon sellers | Make product photos look catalog-ready without a studio or a reshoot | Phone shot → studio shot: backdrop, lighting, propping appear — **the product itself never changes** | Amateur phone photo of one product on a cluttered home surface (dull, flat light) |
| **fashion** | Boutiques, apparel brands, stylists | Get lookbook variety without booking a second shoot | Restyle / add a piece / swap a colorway while the **garment and the person stay true** | Clean outfit-on-model or flat-lay shot, one look |
| **creative-teams** | In-house / agency creative teams | Push a concept to client-ready in-house, with finesse | Layer + mask + brush control on a composite that needs precision — **the hero object never moves** | A product concept render or prototype shot |
| **creatives** | Concept & VFX artists | Turn a sketch into finished art, region by region | Select a region, name a direction, Skill Strength polish→redesign — **every pass on its own layer, over the sketch** | A line sketch / concept lineart |
| **spaces** | Real-estate agents, home stagers | Stage a listing without physically staging it | Furnish, light, dress a room — **the room stays the real room** | Empty or bare room, phone-quality listing photo |
| **photos** | Everyday people | Rescue the almost-perfect shot | Fix one thing, keep the memory — **the subject never changes** | A candid good photo with one fixable flaw or distraction |

## Picking the edits (the reveals)

Each reel is 2–3 reveals. Make them tell the transformation in order of impact:

1. **The hero move first** — the biggest, most valuable change (new backdrop, the restyle, the staging). This is the reveal that makes someone stop scrolling.
2. **Then the finishing touches** — the smaller edits that show *control and range* (light the wick, recolour the bag, add the flowers).

The founder rule holds: the brush **selection** should always read bigger than the change, and the change should sit *inside* a generous selection — so keep edits confidently sized, not fiddly. See `INDUSTRY_CASES` in `refineCase.ts` for the shipped examples per niche.
