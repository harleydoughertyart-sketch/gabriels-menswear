Quick answers before you stop in.

Is my fitting confirmed when I book?

Yes. You’ll see your confirmation right away, and a copy comes by email. To change the time, call me at (508) 829-5186.

Can I bring a suit I own?

Yes. Bring it in. I’ll check the fit and tell you whether it’s worth altering.

Need it for a specific date?

Call me with your date. I’ll tell you whether there’s time for the suit and alterations.
