Quick answers before you stop in.

Do I need to know my size?

No. Come in, try on a suit, and I’ll check the fit.

Can I bring a suit I own?

Yes. Bring it in. I’ll check the fit and tell you whether it’s worth altering.

What will it cost?

The garment, fabric and alterations affect the price. I’ll explain the cost before you decide.

What if prom is soon?

Call me with the prom date. I’ll tell you whether there’s time for the suit and alterations.
