This website helps you find the shop, explore suit fittings and alterations, and get in touch.

Booking a fitting

Booking through the fitting form sends your details through FormSubmit and books the date and time you choose within shop hours. You’ll see a confirmation page and get a confirmation email. To change or cancel, call the shop at (508) 829-5186. If your event is close, call to check there’s time for alterations.

Prices, availability and timing

Discuss the garment, price and deadline with Gabriel before deciding on any work. Website images do not confirm that a particular suit or accessory is available. Call to check opening hours before making a special trip.

Shop purchases and alterations

Ask the shop about any terms that apply to your purchase or alteration before agreeing to it. This page does not set return, cancellation, shipping or payment policies.

Reviews and outside websites

Customer quotes describe those customers’ experiences. They are not a promise of the same timing or result. Links to Google and other providers lead to websites with their own terms.
